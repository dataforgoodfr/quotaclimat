# Stage 5: enriching

Data enrichment of the classified ads, one sub-folder per step, each with its own entry point:

- `e01_brand_groups`: group of the broadcast brands, proposed in the `Inventaires_des_marques` Google Sheet (`entrypoints/advertising_brand_groups.sh`).
- `e02_screenshots`: mosaics of frames of every ad and occurrence, to check the ads and their metadata by eye in Metabase (`entrypoints/advertising_screenshots.sh`).
